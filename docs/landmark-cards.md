# 骨性標記資訊卡（Bone／Description／Attachments／Related）

由資訊卡程式輸出整理，供審核。「附著構造」是把原有的作用句子中含 origin／insertion／attachment 的片段歸入；其餘歸入「相關構造」。右側為例，左側同名。

## Right greater tubercle of humerus / 右肱骨大結節
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: Lateral, at the upper end of the humerus below the head; carries the supraspinatus, infraspinatus and teres minor attachments ｜ 位於肱骨上端、肱骨頭外下方；供棘上肌、棘下肌與小圓肌附著
- Attachments｜附著構造: Insertion of three rotator cuff muscles ｜ 三條旋轉肌袖肌肉的止點
## Right lesser tubercle of humerus / 右肱骨小結節
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: Anterior, medial to the greater tubercle; carries the subscapularis tendon ｜ 位於前方、大結節內側；供肩胛下肌腱附著
- Attachments｜附著構造: Insertion of subscapularis ｜ 肩胛下肌的止點
## Right intertubercular groove / 右結節間溝
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: Between the greater and lesser tubercles on the front of the humerus; also called the bicipital groove ｜ 位於肱骨前面大、小結節之間；又稱二頭肌溝
- Related structures｜相關構造: Holds the tendon of the long head of biceps brachii ｜ 容納肱二頭肌長頭肌腱
## Right surgical neck of humerus / 右肱骨外科頸
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: Narrowing just below the tubercles, where fractures are common ｜ 結節下方的縮窄處，是常見骨折部位
- Related structures｜相關構造: Axillary nerve and the posterior circumflex humeral vessels wind round it ｜ 腋神經與旋肱後血管繞過此處
## Right radial groove / 右橈神經溝
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: A spiral groove running obliquely on the back of the humeral shaft, from medial above to lateral below ｜ 在肱骨幹後面斜行的螺旋溝，由上內側走向下外側
- Related structures｜相關構造: Carries the radial nerve and the deep brachial artery ｜ 容納橈神經與肱深動脈
## Right medial epicondyle of humerus / 右肱骨內上髁
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the humerus ｜ 肱骨
- Description｜描述: Prominent bump on the medial side of the lower end of the humerus ｜ 肱骨下端內側的明顯隆起
- Attachments｜附著構造: Common flexor origin ｜ 屈肌總起點
- Related structures｜相關構造: the ulnar nerve passes behind it ｜ 尺神經經其後方
## Right coracoid process / 右喙突
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the scapula ｜ 肩胛骨
- Description｜描述: Hook-shaped projection from the upper border of the scapula, pointing forwards and laterally ｜ 肩胛骨上緣向前外方的鉤狀突起
- Attachments｜附著構造: Origin of coracobrachialis and the short head of biceps; insertion of pectoralis minor ｜ 喙肱肌與肱二頭肌短頭的起點；胸小肌的止點
## Right suprascapular notch / 右肩胛上切跡
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the scapula ｜ 肩胛骨
- Description｜描述: A notch in the upper border of the scapula, medial to the base of the coracoid process, bridged by the superior transverse scapular ligament ｜ 肩胛骨上緣、喙突基部內側的切跡，上方由肩胛上橫韌帶橫跨
- Related structures｜相關構造: The suprascapular nerve passes through it; the suprascapular artery runs above the ligament ｜ 肩胛上神經由此通過；肩胛上動脈走在韌帶上方
## Right infraglenoid tubercle / 右盂下結節
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the scapula ｜ 肩胛骨
- Description｜描述: Rough area just below the glenoid cavity ｜ 關節盂正下方的粗糙面
- Attachments｜附著構造: Origin of the long head of triceps brachii ｜ 肱三頭肌長頭的起點
## Right infraspinous fossa / 右棘下窩
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the scapula ｜ 肩胛骨
- Description｜描述: The large hollow below the spine of the scapula on its back surface ｜ 肩胛骨後面、肩胛棘下方的大凹窩
- Attachments｜附著構造: Origin of infraspinatus ｜ 棘下肌的起點
- Related structures｜相關構造: the suprascapular vessels and nerve supply it ｜ 由肩胛上血管與神經供應
## Right styloid process of radius / 右橈骨莖突
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the radius ｜ 橈骨
- Description｜描述: Pointed lateral projection at the lower end of the radius ｜ 橈骨下端外側的尖突
- Attachments｜附著構造: Insertion of brachioradialis; the radial collateral ligament of the wrist attaches here ｜ 肱橈肌的止點；腕橈側副韌帶附著於此
## Right iliac crest / 右髂嵴
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the hip bone ｜ 髖骨
- Description｜描述: The curved upper border of the ilium, from the anterior to the posterior superior iliac spine ｜ 髂骨彎曲的上緣，由髂前上棘延伸到髂後上棘
- Attachments｜附著構造: Attachment of the abdominal wall muscles, latissimus dorsi and the fascia lata ｜ 腹壁肌肉、背闊肌與闊筋膜的附著處
## Right pubic tubercle / 右恥骨結節
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the hip bone ｜ 髖骨
- Description｜描述: A small rounded projection on the upper border of the pubis, about 2 cm from the symphysis ｜ 恥骨上緣、距恥骨聯合約 2 公分的小圓突
- Attachments｜附著構造: Medial attachment of the inguinal ligament ｜ 腹股溝韌帶的內側附著點
## Right ischial tuberosity / 右坐骨結節
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the hip bone ｜ 髖骨
- Description｜描述: Thick roughened lower part of the ischium that bears the weight in sitting ｜ 坐骨下方粗糙肥厚、坐姿時承重的部分
- Attachments｜附著構造: Origin of the hamstrings; sacrotuberous ligament attaches here ｜ 膕繩肌的起點；薦結節韌帶附著於此
## Right spine of ischium / 右坐骨棘
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the hip bone ｜ 髖骨
- Description｜描述: Pointed projection from the back of the ischium between the greater and lesser sciatic notches ｜ 坐骨後緣、坐骨大切跡與小切跡之間的尖突
- Attachments｜附著構造: Attachment of the sacrospinous ligament ｜ 薦棘韌帶的附著處
- Related structures｜相關構造: the pudendal nerve and internal pudendal vessels wind round it ｜ 陰部神經與陰部內血管繞過此處
## Right tibial tuberosity / 右脛骨粗隆
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the tibia ｜ 脛骨
- Description｜描述: Rough elevation on the front of the upper tibia ｜ 脛骨上端前面的粗糙隆起
- Attachments｜附著構造: Insertion of the patellar ligament ｜ 髕韌帶的止點
## Mastoid process / 乳突
- Bone｜所在骨骼: On the temporal bone, behind the ear ｜ 顳骨上，耳後方
- Description｜描述: A conical projection behind the external acoustic meatus ｜ 外耳道後方的圓錐形突起
- Attachments｜附著構造: Attachment of sternocleidomastoid, splenius capitis and longissimus capitis ｜ 胸鎖乳突肌、頭夾肌與頭最長肌的附著處
## Jugular foramen / 頸靜脈孔
- Bone｜所在骨骼: Skull base, between the petrous part of the temporal bone and the occipital bone ｜ 顱底，顳骨岩部與枕骨之間
- Description｜描述: Large opening behind the carotid canal ｜ 頸動脈管後方的大孔
- Related structures｜相關構造: Transmits the internal jugular vein and cranial nerves IX, X and XI ｜ 通過內頸靜脈與第九、十、十一對腦神經
## Superior nuchal line / 上項線
- Bone｜所在骨骼: Back of the occipital bone ｜ 枕骨後面
- Description｜描述: A curved ridge running laterally from the external occipital protuberance ｜ 由枕外隆凸向兩側延伸的弧形骨嵴
- Attachments｜附著構造: Attachment of trapezius, sternocleidomastoid, splenius capitis and semispinalis capitis ｜ 斜方肌、胸鎖乳突肌、頭夾肌與頭半棘肌的附著處
## Posterior arch of atlas / 寰椎後弓
- Bone｜所在骨骼: Atlas (C1) ｜ 寰椎（C1）
- Description｜描述: The curved bar of bone closing the vertebral foramen of the atlas at the back ｜ 在後方封閉寰椎椎孔的弧形骨條
- Attachments｜附著構造: rectus capitis posterior minor attaches to its posterior tubercle ｜ 頭後小直肌附著於後結節
- Related structures｜相關構造: The vertebral artery lies in a groove on its upper surface ｜ 椎動脈位於其上面的溝內
## Spinous process of axis / 樞椎棘突
- Bone｜所在骨骼: Axis (C2) ｜ 樞椎（C2）
- Description｜描述: Large bifid spinous process, the first palpable spinous process below the skull ｜ 粗大的分叉棘突，是顱骨下方第一個可摸到的棘突
- Attachments｜附著構造: Attachment of obliquus capitis inferior, rectus capitis posterior major and semispinalis cervicis ｜ 頭下斜肌、頭後大直肌與頸半棘肌的附著處
