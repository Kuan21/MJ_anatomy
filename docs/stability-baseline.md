# Stability baseline

Generated by `node scripts/check-stability.mjs --write`. Only structure names and numbers. Each line is a known violation registered in `docs/stability-known.json` (with its reason); the list may only shrink.

Checks and thresholds (rest pose unless stated; lengths in millimetres): fragments = a piece of a mesh at least 8 from every other piece and under half of its size, or a whole structure with no other mesh within 20 to 40; bone penetration = a vertex of a vessel or nerve wall at least 2 deep inside a bone (tendon 3), three or more vertices; z-fighting = faces of two meshes that face the same way, lie within 0.15 of one plane and within 6 of each other, at least 40 faces and 25 % of the smaller mesh; cuts = an open boundary with a straight run of 15 or more, plus the places in the app that set a renderer clipping plane; stretch = a triangle edge that is more than 2 times its rest length (and over 3 once posed) in a limit pose; leaving the bone = at least 30 % of the points of a vessel, nerve, tendon or ligament that touched a moving bone are 15 or more further from it than at rest.

Poses: shoulder flexion 150, shoulder abduction 120, elbow flexion 140, forearm rotation to both ends (80), hip flexion 120 with knee flexion 130, head flexion 40 and extension 35.
