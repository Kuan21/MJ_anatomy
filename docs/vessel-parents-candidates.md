# Candidate parents worked out from the meshes (not used by the viewer)

Generated by `node scripts/build-vessel-parents.mjs` (do not edit). For arteries and veins that no table gives a parent, the thicker structure of the same kind on the same side that one of their ends touches (within 2 mm) is listed here as a *candidate*.
The rule is only a guess from the shapes, and many of the guesses are wrong (a vein that really drains into the external iliac vein is listed under the deep femoral vein, the superior vena cava under a brachiocephalic vein), so **nothing here is shown on the site**. A line the user confirms is added to `scripts/data/vessel-parent-links.json` with the textbook fact it states, and then the viewer uses it.
