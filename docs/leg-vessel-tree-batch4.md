## 建法

1. 每條血管以網站現有網格（圖譜或示意）的中心線為導引，保留位置；沒有可用導引的，依骨與肌網格上量到的標誌另寫路線（示意）。
2. 起點移到上一級的中心線上，子血管一定接在上一級上；吻合支的兩端各接在一條血管上。
3. 中心線被推出骨外（壁離骨面至少 1.2 mm）並避開神經，再平滑；直徑由起端線性遞減到末端，只有標記為末端的血管才收細。

## 資訊卡欄位（由血管樹產生）

### Medial plantar artery｜足底內側動脈

- Origin 起點：Posterior tibial artery, deep to the flexor retinaculum｜脛後動脈，屈肌支持帶深面
- Course 走向：The smaller terminal branch: runs forwards along the medial side of the sole deep to abductor hallucis and continues along the medial border of the big toe｜較細的終支，沿足底內側在拇展肌深面向前，延續為大趾內側緣的固有趾足底動脈
- Main branches 主要分支：Medial proper plantar digital artery of big toe（大趾內側固有趾足底動脈）
- Territory 供應範圍：Muscles and skin of the medial sole and the big toe｜足底內側的肌肉與皮膚、大趾

### Lateral plantar artery｜足底外側動脈

- Origin 起點：Posterior tibial artery, deep to the flexor retinaculum｜脛後動脈，屈肌支持帶深面
- Course 走向：The larger terminal branch: runs obliquely laterally between flexor digitorum brevis and quadratus plantae to the base of the fifth metatarsal, then curves medially as the plantar arch｜較粗的終支，在屈趾短肌與蹠方肌之間斜向外側，到第五蹠骨底，再向內彎成足底弓
- Main branches 主要分支：Plantar arch（足底弓）、Lateral proper plantar digital artery of little toe（小趾外側固有趾足底動脈）
- Territory 供應範圍：Muscles of the lateral and central sole and the little toe｜足底外側與中央的肌肉、小趾

### Deep plantar artery｜深蹠動脈

- Origin 起點：Dorsalis pedis artery, at the proximal end of the first interosseous space｜足背動脈，第一蹠骨間隙近端
- Course 走向：Dips between the two heads of the first dorsal interosseous muscle into the sole and joins the lateral plantar artery to complete the plantar arch｜穿過第一背側骨間肌兩頭之間入足底，與足底外側動脈會合成足底弓
- Main branches 主要分支：Plantar arch（足底弓）
- Territory 供應範圍：Plantar arch; the first plantar metatarsal territory｜足底弓；第一蹠區

### Plantar arch｜足底弓

- Origin 起點：Lateral plantar artery (completed medially by the deep plantar artery)｜足底外側動脈（內側由深蹠動脈接成）
- Course 走向：Curves medially across the bases of the metatarsal bones deep to the long flexor tendons and the interossei and joins the deep plantar artery｜在長屈肌腱與骨間肌深面橫過蹠骨底向內側，與深蹠動脈會合
- Main branches 主要分支：Plantar metatarsal artery 1（第一足底蹠動脈）、Plantar metatarsal artery 2（第二足底蹠動脈）、Plantar metatarsal artery 3（第三足底蹠動脈）、Plantar metatarsal artery 4（第四足底蹠動脈）
- Territory 供應範圍：Plantar metatarsal arteries and the deep muscles of the sole｜足底蹠動脈與足底深層肌肉

### Plantar metatarsal artery 1｜第一足底蹠動脈

- Origin 起點：Plantar arch｜足底弓
- Course 走向：Runs forwards in the first interosseous space on the plantar side of the interossei and divides at the cleft between toes 1 and 2｜沿第一蹠骨間隙在骨間肌足底側向前，於第1、2趾間分叉
- Main branches 主要分支：Medial proper plantar digital artery of second toe（第二趾內側固有趾足底動脈）、Lateral proper plantar digital artery of big toe（大趾外側固有趾足底動脈）
- Territory 供應範圍：Interossei and the adjacent sides of two toes｜骨間肌與相鄰兩趾的相對面

### Plantar metatarsal artery 2｜第二足底蹠動脈

- Course 走向：Runs along the lateral dorsal border of the big toe to the middle of the proximal phalanx｜沿大趾外側背緣到近端趾節中部
- Main branches 主要分支：無（本批範圍內）
- Territory 供應範圍：Skin of the dorsum of the big toe｜大趾背側皮膚
